Teen Makes Custom Crochet Gifts

A local teen recently started a crochet business from home. She plans to
sell handmade items made with different colors and types of yarn.
She will make things like bags, plushies, flowers, keychains, and more!

How She Started Crochet

She first started crocheting a couple years ago but stopped because it was
too difficult. She then started back up again after being motivated by her
friends, and other people around her. She started by learning the basics from
one of her close family friends and was soon making things like small turtles
and little octopuses. She soon reached the level to where she was making bags
for her family.

What She Crochets

Some of her favorite things to make are hats and plushies. She also enjoys
making smaller projects like flowers and keychains because they take less time.
She likes to make her crochet items with different patterns to see what works best
and turns out better when finished. She takes custom orders from family and
friends but not other clients because she hasn't fully reached the level to go
completely detailed.

Family Support

Her mother is really excited about the new business.I see my daughter's
business as a really good revenue stream and am very proud of her
for finding something she enjoys, is good at, and is turning it into a business.
Her mother hopes the business continues to grow.

Why Crochet Is Exciting

She believes crochet is exciting because it allows you to use your creativity and imagination
to create unique designs and patterns. Crochet is a way to share your creations with other people.
Another exciting thing about crochet is that you never know everything and always get a chance
to learn something. The whole process of crochet isn't relaxing but it is in a way peaceful in a
kind of way

Looking Ahead

The new crochet business gives her a chance to turn her hobby into something bigger. She hopes to
continue creating new items and growing her business. With her creativity and love for crochet, she is
excited to see what comes next.
